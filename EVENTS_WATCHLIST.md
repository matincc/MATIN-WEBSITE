# Events watchlist · Sources à surveiller

Internal file, not published on the site. A short list of trusted sources to check each week
for events that fit Matin's agenda (see `GUIDE_ADMIN.md`, "Ajouter un événement à l'agenda").

These are places to **look**, not to copy: confirm every event on the organizer's own page,
write Matin's own summary, credit the organizer and link to the official page. None of these
organizations is a partner of Matin; never present them as one. No feed, API or reuse
permission has been checked yet (column "Feed"); until one is confirmed, entries are added
by hand.

## Selection questions

1. Does the event relate to Matin's mission (intercultural dialogue, cultural policy and
   planning, cities and public space, community participation, research, relevant arts)?
2. Is the organizer credible?
3. Is the information current and verifiable on the organizer's page?
4. Is it useful to audiences in Montréal and Québec? For a distant event, is its wider
   relevance clear?

Avoid general entertainment listings and festivals unrelated to the mission.

## Suggested balance (a guide, not a quota)

About 75% Montréal and Québec, 15% the rest of Canada, 10% international.

## Later (phase 3)

When a source offers an official RSS, iCal or API feed and its terms allow reuse, it can feed
a candidate list for review. Nothing from a feed is ever published without a person checking
it first.
